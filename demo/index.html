WHY WE BUILT IT

A shop is more than
a checkout button.

Behind each purchase, several systems have to agree. NexCart is our way of exploring how those systems work together.

What we built

A microservices-based e-commerce platform with six connected services: users, products, carts, orders, payments, and notifications. This storefront makes that platform usable.

The problem we wanted to explore

What happens when stock runs low, a payment is retried, or an order fails halfway through? We built NexCart to handle those cases with authorization, stock reservations, and recovery.

Who it is for

Developers learning distributed commerce and reviewers who want to try a complete shopping flow. It is a working demonstration, with a path toward deployment once infrastructure and real providers are configured.
